* Shape retains opcode, registers, stack offsets, literals and internal branch destinations as function-relative offsets. Only in sync_slots/make_marked are the audited TLS member relocations replaced by MARK/RAW_BIAS symbols. Equality of instruction shapes does not establish equality of data addresses, indirect driver dispatches, hardware counters or wall-clock cost.
** Matching direct target means at least one identical PDB alias for paired direct external call/branch instructions at the same instruction ordinal when shape matches; ICF may produce multiple legitimate aliases.
